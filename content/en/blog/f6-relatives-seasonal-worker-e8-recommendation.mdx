# Bringing a Relative From Home for Seasonal Farm or Fishery Work in Korea — How a Marriage Migrant Recommends Relatives up to the Fourth Degree (E-8-2, E-8-4)

A marriage migrant staying in Korea can recommend a relative living abroad within the fourth degree of kinship (including that relative's spouse) to a Korean local government as a seasonal worker. The local government that receives the recommendation assigns the relative to a farm or fishery household and handles the visa issuance confirmation application, and the relative enters on Seasonal Work status (E-8-2 farming or E-8-4 fishing) and works for up to 8 months. The key point is that the marriage migrant does not apply for the visa directly.

## Key Summary

- Program: the "foreign seasonal worker program", which allows foreigners to be employed legally for 8 months to ease labour shortages in farming and fishing seasons
- Recommender: a marriage migrant staying in Korea (including one who acquired Korean nationality)
- Who can be recommended: a relative living abroad within the fourth degree of kinship (including that relative's spouse)
- Status: E-8-2 for farming, E-8-4 for fishing (single-entry visa)
- Applicant: the head of the city, county or district (basic local government) applies for the visa issuance confirmation through the Visa Portal
- Stay: after entry, work in farming or fishing for up to 8 months (maximum 8 months per grant, total stay cannot exceed 8 months)

## 1. Can a marriage migrant bring relatives from home as seasonal workers?

Yes. Among the seasonal foreign workers a local government can bring in under the foreign seasonal worker program is "family living abroad (up to fourth degree) of marriage migrants residing in the local government's area". A marriage migrant staying in Korea (including one who acquired Korean nationality) recommends their relative living abroad (within the fourth degree, including that relative's spouse) to a Korean local government. Where and in what form to recommend, and whether the local government runs this route at all, differ by local government, so check with the local government where you live first.

## 2. What happens after the recommendation?

1. The marriage migrant recommends the relative living abroad to a Korean local government
2. The local government assigns the seasonal worker to a farm household (E-8-2) or fishery household (E-8-4)
3. The local government handles the invitation (visa issuance confirmation application)
4. After entry, the seasonal worker works in farming or fishing for up to 8 months

The Seasonal Work (E-8) visa is issued only on the basis of a visa issuance confirmation issued by the head of the immigration office, and the confirmation is applied for by the head of the city, county or district through the Visa Portal. The total number for each local government is allocated by an allocation review council taking into account management capacity and measures against absconding and human rights violations, and the local government then assigns workers to farm and fishery households under its own criteria. A recommendation therefore does not settle an assignment; the outcome depends on the local government's allocation and screening.

## 3. What work is allowed?

The permitted sectors are farming and fishing that need concentrated labour for 8 months due to seasonality. The marriage migrant recommendation route is divided into E-8-2 for farming and E-8-4 for fishing, so the recommender and permitted sector must be checked, the correct visa type applied for and the required information entered accurately. Issuance of the former short-term seasonal work (C-4) visa was discontinued from 2025.

## 4. Which documents does the marriage migrant prepare?

Among the documents attached on the Visa Portal for the visa issuance confirmation, those concerning a marriage migrant's relative within the fourth degree (including spouse) are:
- The marriage migrant's ID
- Marriage relation certificate (domestic)
- Family relation certificate (domestic) and basic certificate (for those naturalized through marriage)
- A kinship chart of the marriage migrant's relatives
- A family relation certificate issued in the country of residence

The family (kinship) relationship with the marriage migrant in Korea must be proven, and documents in a foreign language other than English must be accompanied by a translator's confirmation and translation (notarization not required). Other attachments, such as the standard employment contract, proof of efforts to recruit Korean workers, a copy of the seasonal worker's passport and the accommodation details for the seasonal worker, are also attached, and who prepares each one follows the local government's guidance. It is important to arrange the kinship chart and family documents so that the fourth-degree relationship is connected without gaps.

## 5. Can family already in Korea do seasonal work this way?

Under this recommendation route, the visa issuance confirmation covers foreigners staying abroad who are assigned to employers by the local government. Family of marriage migrants who are already staying in Korea are not covered by the visa issuance confirmation; they apply for permission for activities outside their status instead. What conditions apply for family in Korea to obtain that permission depends on their current status and needs to be checked case by case. For reference, the Marriage Migrant (F-6) holder personally is not restricted in employment by status category.

## 6. What restrictions apply to the relative after entry?

- Period of stay: the maximum that can be granted at one time is 8 months, and the total stay cannot exceed 8 months.
- Foreigner registration: integrated application form, passport, photo, fee, confirmation of residence or accommodation provided, travel insurance certificate, drug test certificate (submitted sealed) and so on.
- Workplace: adding a workplace is not allowed, and changing workplace is limited to cases such as the employment contract being terminated midway without fault on the seasonal worker's part.
- Other activities: seasonal workers (E-8) are not granted permission for activities other than seasonal work.

## 7. Common problems

- Assuming the marriage migrant applies for the visa directly and missing the local government recommendation step
- A kinship chart and home-country family documents that do not show the fourth-degree relationship in a continuous chain
- Foreign-language documents without a translator's confirmation and translation
- Choosing farming (E-8-2) or fishing (E-8-4) incorrectly so the visa type does not match
- Believing the relative can do other work or stay beyond 8 months after entry

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does the marriage migrant apply for the relative's seasonal work visa directly?

No. The marriage migrant recommends the relative to a Korean local government, and the head of the city, county or district applies for the visa issuance confirmation through the Visa Portal.

### Which relatives can be recommended?

Relatives living abroad within the fourth degree of kinship, including that relative's spouse.

### How long can the relative work in Korea?

After entry, they work in farming or fishing for up to 8 months, and the total stay cannot exceed 8 months.

### Can a marriage migrant who acquired Korean nationality recommend?

Yes. Marriage migrants who can recommend include those who acquired Korean nationality.

### Can the relative do other work during seasonal work?

Seasonal workers (E-8) are not granted permission for activities other than seasonal work, and adding a workplace is not allowed.

# Lost or Damaged Residence Card? — Grounds for Reissue, Where to Apply and Documents for Marriage Migrants

When you complete foreigner registration on Marriage Migrant (F-6) status, you receive a residence card (alien registration card). If you lose it or it becomes worn out and unusable, you can apply for reissue at the immigration office with jurisdiction over your place of stay. The grounds for reissue are set by law, and whether you must hand in the original card depends on the ground. This guide is for marriage migrants who need a new card.

## Key Summary

- Grounds for reissue: loss, worn out and unusable, not enough space for entries, permission to change status, report of change of personal details, batch renewal for purposes such as preventing forgery
- Where: the immigration office (office or branch office) with jurisdiction over your place of stay
- Documents: integrated application form (select "Reissuance of registration card"), 1 photo, and the original residence card for grounds other than loss
- Registration number: reissue due to loss, damage and similar grounds keeps the existing alien registration number
- Mobile residence card: reissue of the physical card is a ground for cancelling the existing mobile card, and the mobile card can be reissued
- Fee: charged, but waived when reissue is due to an error in issuance

## 1. When can a card be reissued?

The head of the immigration office, office or branch office with jurisdiction over the place of stay may reissue a residence card to a person who has received one when any of the following grounds apply.

| Ground | Attach original card |
|---|---|
| The residence card was lost | Not applicable |
| The residence card is worn out and unusable | Attach |
| There is not enough space for entries on the card | Attach |
| Permission to change status of stay was granted | Attach |
| A report of change of name, sex, date of birth or nationality was received | Attach |
| Cards need to be renewed all at once, for example to prevent forgery | Attach |

Being granted a change of status from another status to Marriage Migrant (F-6), or filing a report of change of alien registration details after your name changed in your home country, also leads to a card reissue. Cases outside these grounds need to be checked case by case.

## 2. If you lost your card

Loss is the only ground for which you do not have to attach the original card. Attach 1 photo to the reissue application and submit it to the immigration office with jurisdiction over your place of stay. The deadline for applying after a loss needs to be checked case by case, but since you need a way to prove your identity while you have no card, it is better to apply quickly.

Foreigners staying in Korea must always carry their passport, residence card, mobile residence card or similar (except those under 17). While your card is lost, you can carry your passport, or use your mobile residence card if you have been issued one.

## 3. If the card is worn out or has no space left for entries

When the card is worn out and unusable, or has run out of space for entries, attach the original residence card to the reissue application. When you report a change of place of stay after moving, the office writes the change on your card and returns it, so moving several times can use up the space. The office destroys the original card received at reissue.

## 4. Where to apply and what to submit

- Where: the immigration office (office or branch office) with jurisdiction over your place of stay
- Form: select "Reissuance of registration card" on the integrated application form (Form No. 34)
- Photo: 1 passport photo (35mm×45mm) taken within the last 6 months. On the integrated application form, a photo is attached only for foreigner registration and card reissue.
- Original card: attach for grounds other than loss
- Fee: a reissue fee is charged; check the amount and payment method with the office with jurisdiction before applying. The fee is waived when reissue is due to an error in issuing the card.

When the card is reissued because of loss, damage, lack of space, permission to change status or batch renewal, the existing alien registration number continues to be used. The number changes only after a report of change of sex or date of birth. The reissue application and collection can be done by you or through a person designated by the Minister of Justice, and collecting a reissued residence card is among the tasks a registered agency may handle on your behalf.

## 5. What happens to the mobile residence card?

The mobile residence card is a card installed in encrypted form on a mobile phone and has the same effect as the residence card. When the physical card is reissued, that is a ground for cancelling the existing mobile residence card, and the mobile card can also be reissued. Conversely, losing the phone on which the mobile card is installed, or the phone becoming unusable, is also a ground for cancelling the mobile card, and it can be reissued when it can no longer be used.

To be issued or reissued a mobile residence card, submit the application on Form No. 34-9 to the office with jurisdiction over your place of stay and present your passport or residence card. If you have lost the physical card, prepare your passport.

## 6. Points to note when leaving and re-entering Korea

As a rule, a registered foreigner returns the residence card to the immigration officer when departing. However, the card is not returned in cases such as departing temporarily with a re-entry permit and re-entering within the permit period, or, as a multiple-visa holder or a national of a country exempt from re-entry permits, re-entering within the permitted period of stay.

How departure and re-entry are handled at the airport while your card is lost needs to be checked case by case. If you have a trip planned and lose your card, first check with the immigration office with jurisdiction whether to apply for reissue before departure and what procedures are needed when leaving.

## 7. Common mistakes

- Not bringing the original card for a ground other than loss, delaying the application
- Not preparing a photo, or submitting one taken more than 6 months ago
- Getting a new physical card and forgetting to deal with the mobile card on the phone
- Leaving the card with a workplace or someone else and having it lost (receiving a passport or residence card as security for a debt is prohibited by law)
- Looking into what to do only just before departure after losing the card

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I get a reissue without the original card if I lost it?

Yes. Loss is a ground for which the original card does not need to be attached. Attach 1 photo to the reissue application and submit it to the immigration office with jurisdiction over your place of stay.

### Does my alien registration number change on reissue?

Reissue because of loss, damage, lack of space, permission to change status or batch renewal keeps the existing number. A new number is assigned only for reissue following a report of change of sex or date of birth.

### My card is so worn the text is hard to read. Can it be reissued?

A residence card that is worn out and unusable is a ground for reissue. In this case, attach the original card to the reissue application.

### If I have a mobile residence card, do I still need to reissue the physical card?

The mobile residence card has the same effect as the physical card. However, reissuing the physical card is a ground for cancelling the existing mobile card, so how to manage the physical and mobile cards together needs to be checked case by case.

### Does everyone pay the reissue fee?

A fee is charged for reissue. It is waived when reissue is due to an error in issuing the card; check the amount and payment method with the office with jurisdiction.

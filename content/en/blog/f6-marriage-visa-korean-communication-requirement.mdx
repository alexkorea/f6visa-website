# F-6 Marriage Visa Korean Communication Requirement: Is TOPIK Level 1 Mandatory?

The F-6-1 communication requirement does not rely on TOPIK Level 1 alone. You choose one document listed in the manual, such as a Korean course certificate, a King Sejong Institute certificate, a TOPIK Level 1 or higher score, a designated institution certificate, or proof of the couple's residence history; couples with a child together, or where the foreign spouse previously held F-6 status, may be exempt from submitting these documents.

## Key Summary

- Submit one of the listed documents: Korean Education Center or King Sejong Institute certificate, TOPIK Level 1+, designated institution certificate, degree, etc.
- If Korean is not the common language, there are routes using the shared language (residence history, language test score, embassy interview)
- A child born to the couple, or past F-6 residence, can mean exemption (not if the spouse changed or the marriage was interrupted)
- If neither exempt nor documented, the Korean mission's assessment decides

## 1. What is the communication requirement?

It checks that the couple can communicate with each other. The manual tells applicants to submit one of the listed documents as proof of the foreign spouse's communication ability.

## 2. Which documents are accepted?

- Korean Education Center Korean course (level 2) certificate, or King Sejong Institute Beginner 1A+1B certificate (at least 120 hours)
- TOPIK Level 1 or higher score report (TOPIK IBT included)
- Certificate from a Korean education institution designated for the country
- Degree from a Korean-related university or graduate school
- Proof of overseas Korean status (language ability may be checked if needed)
- Documents showing the foreign spouse has stayed in Korea continuously for 1 year or more

## 3. Can I prove it in a language other than Korean?

- Proof that the Korean spouse stayed 1 year or more in a country where the foreign spouse's language is official, or proof that the Korean spouse is a naturalized citizen from such a country
- For other languages, proof that the couple stayed 1 year or more in a country where that language is official
- A qualifying score in a language test, or passing an embassy interview conducted in that language

## 4. Who is exempt?

Couples with a child born between them, and cases where the foreign spouse previously stayed in Korea under F-6 status (long or short term), do not need to submit communication documents. This does not apply if the spouse changed, or if the marriage was interrupted even with the same spouse.

## 5. What if I have no document?

If you are not exempt and cannot submit any of the listed documents, the result of the assessment conducted by the Korean mission decides whether the requirement is met.

## 6. Points often missed

- Not checking that the certificate shows the hours (120 or more) and level
- Submitting a score report without checking its date and validity
- Leaving out the entry/exit fact certificate or handwritten statement when proving by residence history
- Not preparing family relation records to prove the exemption (child or past F-6)

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I get a marriage visa without TOPIK Level 1?

Yes, other options exist: Korean Education Center or King Sejong Institute certificates, designated institution certificates, residence history proof. If not exempt, you must meet the requirement through documents or the mission's assessment.

### Which King Sejong Institute course counts?

The manual lists the Beginner 1A+1B certificate (at least 120 hours).

### If we have a child, do we skip the Korean language documents?

A child born to the couple exempts the communication documents. You may need to prove it, for example with the child's family relation certificate.

### Does the Korean spouse's time abroad count?

Proof that the Korean spouse stayed 1 year or more in a country where the foreign spouse's language is official is listed as accepted.

### When is the embassy interview used?

When you are not exempt and cannot submit the listed documents, the mission's assessment applies; passing an interview in the relevant language can also satisfy the requirement.

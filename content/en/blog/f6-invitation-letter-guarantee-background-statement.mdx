# F-6 Marriage Visa Invitation Letter, Letter of Guarantee and Marriage Background Statement: Who Writes What and Who Is Responsible?

Among the documents for a Spouse of a Korean National (F-6-1) visa, the documents you fill in yourself are described as the most important, and they must be completed in full on the prescribed forms. The invitation letter for a foreign spouse and the letter of guarantee are written by the Korean spouse, and the marriage background statement by the foreign spouse. Because the sponsor becomes the identity guarantor of the invited person, the sponsor should understand what the letter of guarantee covers and the guarantee period before signing.

## Key Summary

- Invitation letter for a foreign spouse: written in Korean by the Korean spouse, stating income and assets
- Letter of guarantee: the sponsor is the guarantor; the guarantee period is up to 4 years
- Marriage background statement: written in English by the foreign spouse
- Forms: the embassy and the HiKorea website
- Invitation letters, marriage relation certificates and similar documents must be within 3 months of writing or issuance
- If the foreign spouse previously stayed on F-6 with the same spouse without a break in the marriage, the marriage background statement drops out of the list

## 1. Who writes the invitation letter, and how?

The invitation letter for a foreign spouse is written in Korean by the Korean spouse, on the form available from the embassy and the HiKorea website. The sponsor states income and assets in the invitation letter and submits documents proving them (income certificates issued by the National Tax Service, real estate registry extracts, deposit certificates, employment certificates, bankbook copies and the like). If the international marriage guidance program applies, the application must attach the completion certificate or state the completion number in the invitation letter.

## 2. What does the letter of guarantee cover?

A marriage-cohabitation visa requires an invitation from the spouse, and the sponsor becomes the identity guarantor of the invited person. The letter of guarantee form lists three commitments:

- Ensure that all laws are obeyed during the stay
- Bear responsibility for paying departure travel costs and related expenses
- Bear responsibility for paying costs arising during the stay or while in protective custody

The Immigration Act provides that the guarantor may be made to bear all or part of the costs of the guaranteed foreigner's stay, protection and departure, and that if the guarantor fails to meet this responsibility and the state bears the cost, a right to reimbursement may be exercised against the guarantor.

## 3. How should the guarantee period be written?

The guarantee period in the letter of guarantee is up to 4 years. Even if 4 years or more is written, it is recognized only up to 4 years, and the period of any permit cannot exceed the guarantee period in the letter of guarantee. Writing a short guarantee period can affect later permit periods, so check the period before filling it in.

## 4. Who writes the marriage background statement?

The marriage background statement is written in English by the foreign spouse. How the couple met and the genuineness of the marriage are also shown through the separate relationship evidence, so it is important that the invitation letter, the marriage background statement and the relationship evidence do not contradict each other.

## 5. Are there cases where fewer documents are needed?

For a foreign spouse who has previously stayed in Korea with Marriage Migrant (F-6) status, the basic document list includes only the invitation letter for a foreign spouse and the letter of guarantee, without the marriage background statement. However, this does not apply if the spouse has changed or if the marriage was interrupted even with the same spouse. Also, unless otherwise specified, submitted documents such as the invitation letter are valid for 3 months from the date of writing or issuance, so if the schedule slips you may have to write them again.

## 6. Common problems

- The income and assets stated in the invitation letter do not match the amounts in the supporting documents
- The time and place of meeting differ between the invitation letter, the marriage background statement and the relationship evidence
- Writing a short guarantee period in the letter of guarantee
- Submitting an invitation letter more than 3 months after it was written

# What Happens to Your Residence Card When You Leave Korea for Good — Returning the Alien Registration Card, Exceptions, Where to Return It and Cancellation of Registration

A marriage migrant who has completed foreigner registration must, as a rule, return the alien registration card (residence card) to an immigration officer when leaving Korea. However, if the departure is a temporary trip with a set return, such as leaving briefly with a re-entry permit, the card is not returned. This guide is for those who are wrapping up life in Korea and going back to their home country, those who have acquired Korean nationality, and those who wonder what to do with the card on a short trip abroad.

## Key Summary

- Rule: a registered foreigner returns the alien registration card to an immigration officer when departing
- When it is not returned: temporary departures set out in the Immigration Act, such as leaving temporarily with a re-entry permit and intending to re-enter within the permit period
- Acquiring nationality: return it to the immigration office with jurisdiction over your place of stay within 30 days of completing resident registration
- Death: a spouse, parent or other person returns it within 30 days of learning of the death, with a document proving the death
- Cancellation of registration: the registration may be cancelled if the card has been returned, or if the person does not enter Korea within the re-entry permit period (or, if exempted, the exemption period or permitted period of stay) after departure

## 1. When must the alien registration card be returned?

The Immigration Act broadly sets out two situations in which the card must be returned. The first is when a registered foreigner departs Korea. The second is when a registered foreigner becomes a Korean national or dies, or comes under a category exempt from foreigner registration. The situations marriage migrants most often face are going back to their home country after wrapping up life in Korea and acquiring Korean nationality through naturalization or otherwise.

## 2. Where do I return it when I leave Korea for good?

A registered foreigner returns the alien registration card to an immigration officer when departing. The immigration officer who receives the card notifies the immigration office with jurisdiction over the place of stay of the departure, and that office notifies the city, county or district and the town or neighbourhood office of the cancellation of foreigner registration. The returned card is destroyed once the procedure is completed.

The same rule applies when a marriage has ended and the person returns to their home country. Make sure to have the card with you on the day of departure so you can return it.

## 3. Which temporary departures do not require returning the card?

The Immigration Act excludes the following temporary departures from the return requirement:
- Leaving temporarily with a re-entry permit and intending to re-enter within the permit period
- Holders of multiple-entry visas, or nationals of countries exempt from re-entry permits, leaving temporarily and intending to re-enter within the permitted period of stay
- Leaving temporarily with a refugee travel document and intending to re-enter within its validity period

Those registered with Marriage Migrant (F-6) status have their own re-entry rules. If they intend to re-enter within 1 year of the date of departure, the re-entry permit is waived (within the period of stay if less than 1 year remains before it expires), and if they intend to re-enter after more than 1 year but within 2 years, they need a multiple re-entry permit. For details, see the re-entry permit exemption guide under Related Guides below. If it is unclear whether your departure falls under an exception, it is safest to check with the immigration office before leaving.

For reference, the head of the immigration office may temporarily keep the card of a foreigner who falls under the exceptions above if this is deemed necessary for the interests of the Republic of Korea. In that case, a person who re-enters within the permitted period must get the card back within 15 days, and a person who does not re-enter within that period is deemed to have returned the card.

## 4. If I acquire Korean nationality, by when must I return it?

When a registered foreigner becomes a Korean national, the person, their spouse, a parent or another eligible person must return the alien registration card to the immigration office (office or branch office) with jurisdiction over the place of stay within 30 days of completing resident registration. Unlike returning it to an immigration officer on departure, in the case of acquiring nationality the card is returned to the competent office. The foreigner registration may be cancelled when the acquisition of Korean nationality is confirmed.

## 5. What if the person dies or becomes exempt from foreigner registration?

- Death: a spouse, parent or other eligible person returns the card to the immigration office with jurisdiction over the place of stay within 30 days of learning of the death, attaching a medical certificate, post-mortem certificate or other document proving the death.
- Becoming exempt from registration: the card is returned to the competent office when a change of status is granted to a status exempt from registration, such as Diplomacy (A-1), Government Official (A-2) or Agreement (A-3).

Failing to meet the return obligation may make you subject to an administrative fine under the Immigration Act, so where a deadline applies, be sure to check the date.

## 6. How is foreigner registration cancelled?

The head of the immigration office may cancel the foreigner registration in the following cases:
- The card was returned on departure: when the card is received
- The person became a Korean national: when the acquisition of nationality is confirmed
- The person died: when the death is confirmed
- The person did not enter Korea within the re-entry permit period (or, if exempted, the exemption period or permitted period of stay) after departure: when that period has passed

It is important to know that even if you took the card with you believing the trip was temporary, your registration may be cancelled if you do not return within the permitted period. A foreigner whose registration was cancelled and who later registers again receives the same foreigner registration number that was assigned before the cancellation.

## 7. Common problems

- Leaving Korea for good while keeping the card instead of returning it
- Leaving for what was meant to be a short trip and having the registration cancelled after exceeding the re-entry permit or exemption period
- Acquiring Korean nationality and completing resident registration but missing the 30-day deadline to return the card
- Planning a departure without knowing the difference in periods between the re-entry permit exemption and the multiple re-entry permit

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where do I hand in my card when I go back to my home country for good?

You return it to an immigration officer when departing.

### Do I return the card when I visit my home country briefly?

If the departure is a temporary one set out in the Immigration Act, such as leaving temporarily with a re-entry permit and intending to re-enter within the permit period, you do not return it. If you are unsure, check before leaving.

### I naturalized and completed resident registration. By when must I return the card?

The person, their spouse, a parent or another eligible person must return it to the immigration office with jurisdiction over the place of stay within 30 days of completing resident registration.

### What happens to my registration if I cannot come back after leaving?

If you do not enter within the re-entry permit period (or, if exempted, the exemption period or permitted period of stay), the foreigner registration may be cancelled when that period has passed.

### If I register again after cancellation, does my number change?

A foreigner whose registration was cancelled and who registers again receives the same foreigner registration number assigned before the cancellation.

# Divorced but Need to Stay? F-6-3 Extension for a Divorce Not Your Fault and the Fault-Evidence Documents

If you divorced for reasons not attributable to you, such as the Korean spouse leaving home or violence, after a normal marriage in F-6-1 status, you can apply for the first stay extension as a marriage-terminated person (F-6-3), granted within a range of 1 year as F-6-3. The file centers on the detailed marriage certificate stating the divorce, divorce-related court documents and evidence of fault; even where the foreigner is at fault, a special rule lets the office examine the facts and proof of support if the foreigner supports the Korean spouse’s parents or family.

## Key Summary

- Eligible: F-6-1 holders who divorced for reasons not attributable to them (national’s leaving home, violence, etc.)
- Core documents: detailed marriage certificate stating the divorce, divorce-related court documents, evidence of fault
- Fault-evidence examples: missing-person report, hospital diagnosis for violence, prosecutor’s non-indictment decision, women’s group confirmation, confirmation from a relative within the 4th degree, neighborhood head confirmation
- Grant: within a range of 1 year as F-6-3

## 1. Does divorce mean I cannot stay?

The manual provides an F-6-3 route for marriage-terminated persons (death, missing, divorce). For divorce, a person who divorced “for reasons not attributable to themselves” is eligible for the first stay extension.

## 2. Who is covered?

A person who stayed as the spouse of a Korean national (F-6-1) in a normal marriage in Korea and divorced for reasons not attributable to them, such as the national leaving home or violence.

## 3. Which documents are required?

- Application form (Form No. 34), passport and alien registration card, fee
- Detailed marriage certificate stating the divorce
- Divorce-related court documents (divorce judgment, mediation record, recommended settlement decision, statement of reasons for divorce by agreement, etc.)
- Evidence of fault
- Child’s family relation certificate if a child was born to the couple
- Foreigner occupation report and proof of address
- Other documents the office considers necessary

## 4. How is fault proven?

Objective materials showing the marriage ended mainly because of the Korean spouse’s fault are needed. Examples are the spouse’s missing-person report, a hospital diagnosis for injuries from violence, a prosecutor’s non-indictment decision, a confirmation from a recognized women’s organization, a confirmation from a relative within the 4th degree of the Korean spouse, and a confirmation from the neighborhood head where you lived when the marriage broke down.

## 5. Is it impossible if I was at fault?

The manual has a special rule: even where the foreigner is at fault, if that person supports the Korean spouse’s parents or family, the office verifies the facts and proof of support before deciding, with a stay granted for a range of 1 year as F-6-3.

## 6. Common problems

- Submitting only court papers with too little material showing fault
- Divorce by agreement leaving few documents and a hard-to-build statement
- Dates of evidence not matching when the marriage broke down

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I stay as F-6-3 after divorce?

If you divorced for reasons not attributable to you, you are eligible for the first marriage-terminated (F-6-3) extension, granted within a range of 1 year.

### What can I submit as fault evidence?

Examples include a missing-person report, a hospital diagnosis for violence, a prosecutor’s non-indictment decision, a women’s group confirmation, a confirmation from a relative within the 4th degree and a neighborhood head confirmation.

### Is divorce by agreement possible?

The divorce-related documents include a statement of reasons for divorce by agreement. Whether it works depends on the reasons and evidence reviewed.

### Is there no option if the divorce was my fault?

Even if the foreigner is at fault, a special rule covers those who support the Korean spouse’s parents or family, where facts and proof of support are checked.

### What extra document applies if there is a child?

The child’s family relation certificate if a child was born to the couple.

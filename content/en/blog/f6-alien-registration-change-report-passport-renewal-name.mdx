# New Passport or Changed Name? — What Marriage Migrants Must Report as a Change of Alien Registration Details, the 15-Day Deadline and What to Bring

A marriage migrant who has completed foreigner registration must file a report of change of alien registration details with the immigration office having jurisdiction over their place of stay within 15 days when certain registered details change. The most common case is receiving a new passport from the home country, and a change of name, sex, date of birth or nationality is also covered. This is a separate procedure from the report of change of place of stay made when you move house, so this guide sets out the difference.

## Key Summary

- Who must report: foreigners who have completed foreigner registration (including Marriage Migrant F-6)
- What to report: name, sex, date of birth and nationality; passport number, issue date and validity period; other matters set by Ministry of Justice ordinance
- Additional items that apply to F-6: a change of occupation or annual income, and a change in school enrolment status
- Deadline: within 15 days of the change
- Where: the immigration office (office or branch office) with jurisdiction over your place of stay
- What to bring: report form for change of alien registration details (integrated application form), residence card, passport, and where applicable documents proving the change
- Moving house is separate: report of change of place of stay within 15 days of moving in

## 1. What is a report of change of alien registration details?

A foreigner who has registered must report to the head of the immigration office with jurisdiction over their place of stay within 15 days when an item set by law among the registered details changes. This is a duty set by the Immigration Act, and it applies equally to those registered on Marriage Migrant (F-6) status. The office that receives the report updates the registered foreigner record and, depending on the item, issues a new residence card.

## 2. Which changes must be reported?

The items the law requires you to report fall into three groups.

| Group | Items to report |
|---|---|
| Personal details | Name, sex, date of birth and nationality |
| Passport details | Passport number, issue date and validity period |
| Other | Matters set by Ministry of Justice ordinance |

Among the matters set by ordinance, those relevant to Marriage Migrant (F-6) are a change of occupation or annual income and a change in school enrolment status. The occupation and annual income item applies to holders of statuses such as Marriage Migrant (F-6) that are not restricted in employment, and school enrolment means enrolment in an elementary, middle or high school. Items that apply only to other statuses, such as a change of affiliated institution (culture and arts, study, etc.) or the start-of-employment report for Working Visit (H-2), have nothing to do with F-6.

## 3. When you get a new passport from your home country

When you renew your passport or receive a new one, the passport number, issue date and validity period all change, so you must report it. If you still have your old passport along with the new one, it is a good idea to keep it as material showing the change. For a foreigner whose passport has been reissued, there is also a procedure in which an immigration officer checks whether the person departed on the old passport and then stamps an entry confirmation in the new passport.

Passport details are also linked to your period of stay. Foreigners on long-term statuses, including Marriage Migrant (F-6), are in principle granted a period of stay within the validity of their passport. Where reissuing the passport is effectively impossible within the remaining period of stay, a period of stay of up to 6 months is granted once only, treating the passport as valid for 6 months even if its remaining validity is 6 months or less. If your passport expires soon before an extension of stay, it is safer to renew the passport first and finish the change report.

## 4. When your name, sex, date of birth or nationality changes

If your personal details have changed, for example you changed your name in your home country or your nationality changed, you also report within 15 days. On receiving a report of change of these personal details, the office reissues the residence card and notifies the head of the city, county or district and the town or neighbourhood office of your place of stay.

The alien registration number is handled differently depending on what changed.
- Change of name or nationality: the existing alien registration number continues to be used
- Change of sex or date of birth: a new alien registration number is assigned

Attach 1 photo to the card reissue application along with the original residence card. If a new registration number is assigned, you may also need to update your details with organizations where you registered the old number, and how each organization handles this needs to be checked case by case.

## 5. By when, where and with what?

- Deadline: within 15 days of the date of change
- Where: the immigration office (office or branch office) with jurisdiction
- Form: select "Change of information on registration" on the integrated application form (Form No. 34)
- Attachments: residence card and passport
- Where applicable: documents proving the change, for a change of personal details such as name and for a change of passport details

Which documents are accepted as proof, such as a name change certificate issued by your home country authority, and whether a translation is needed depend on nationality and circumstances and need to be checked case by case. You can file the report yourself or through a person designated by the Minister of Justice, and the report of change of alien registration details is also among the tasks a registered agency may handle on your behalf. If a child under 17 does not file the report personally, a parent or other designated person must do so. Failing to meet the duty to report a change may make you subject to a fine for negligence.

## 6. If you moved — the report of change of place of stay is separate

Moving house is not a change of alien registration details but is covered by the report of change of place of stay (move-in report). The two procedures compare as follows.

| Item | Change of alien registration details | Change of place of stay |
|---|---|---|
| Reason | Change in registered details such as name or passport | Change of place of stay (address) |
| Deadline | Within 15 days of the change | Within 15 days of moving in |
| Where | Immigration office with jurisdiction over your place of stay | City, county or district or town or neighbourhood office of the new place, or the immigration office with jurisdiction |
| Residence card | Reissued when personal details change | The change is written on the card and returned |

The report of change of place of stay can also be made through the information network designated by the Minister of Justice. If you got a new passport and moved house around the same time, you must make both reports, and the integrated application form has separate boxes for "Change of information on registration" and "Alteration of residence".

## 7. Common mistakes

- Getting a new passport and missing the 15 days, thinking it can wait until the extension of stay
- Reporting only the change of place of stay when moving and forgetting the passport change report
- Not telling other organizations that a new registration number was assigned after a change of sex or date of birth
- A name changed in the home country differing from the name on Korean documents, delaying identity confirmation in other applications
- Missing that parents must report the passport renewal of a child under 17

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I have to report it when I get a new passport?

Yes. The passport number, issue date and validity period are covered by the report of change of alien registration details, so report to the immigration office with jurisdiction over your place of stay within 15 days of the change.

### Will I get a new residence card after the report?

When your name, sex, date of birth or nationality changes, the residence card is reissued. A change of sex or date of birth means a new alien registration number is assigned, while a change of name or nationality keeps the existing number.

### I moved house. Do I file a change of registration details?

No. Moving is covered by the report of change of place of stay, made within 15 days of moving in to the city, county or district or town or neighbourhood office of the new place, or the immigration office with jurisdiction.

### Do I have to report a new job or a change in income?

Holders of statuses not restricted in employment, such as Marriage Migrant (F-6), must also report a change of occupation or annual income as a change of alien registration details.

### What happens if I miss the 15 days?

Failing to meet the duty to report a change of alien registration details may make you subject to a fine for negligence. Report as soon as you notice, even if late, and the specific handling needs to be checked case by case.
